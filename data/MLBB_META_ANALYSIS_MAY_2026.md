## Meta Summary

### **Current Meta Archetypes**
- **Early-Mid Game Dominance**: Diggie, Kaja, Aurora (roam combo)
- **Late Game Scaling**: Beatrix, Claude, Karrie (core marksmen)
- **Team Fight Control**: Kagura, Chou, Arlott (engage/disrupt)
- **Sustain Dependent**: Estes + Masha/Hilda (double sustain)
- **Assassination Threat**: Lancelot, Fanny, Gusion (burst killing)

### **Ban Priority in Ranked**
1. **Khufra** (Game-changing CC chain)
2. **Lancelot** (Unchecked burst threat)
3. **Beatrix** (DPS consistency)
4. **Arlott** (Engage + burst)
5. **Chou** (Control + versatility)

### **Counter-Meta Notes**
- Heavy CC comps (Khufra, Chou, Aurora) counter mobility-heavy assassins
- High-DPS marksmen require reliable protection from supports
- Tank durability stack is critical for securing teamfights
- Estes' healing presence forces teams to commit to burst combos
