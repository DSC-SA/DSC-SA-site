3. **Hollow Radiance** - AoE damage + defense (optional early rotations)
4. **Brute Force Breastplate** - HP + armor scaling
5. **Twilight Armor** - Damage reflection + armor
6. **Immortality** - Late game insurance/second life

**Core Items**: Plated Armor, Brute Force Breastplate, Twilight Armor
**Alternate Items**: Antique Cuirass, Radiant Armor (vs AD heavy), Cursed Helmet (vs magic)

## 5. BUILD ADAPTATION GUIDE

### **When to Adapt Your Build:**

**Against Heavy Physical Damage Teams:**
- Prioritize: Plated Armor → Brute Force Breastplate → Twilight Armor
- Add: Antique Cuirass, Radiant Armor
- Boots: Warrior Boots

**Against Heavy Magic Damage Teams:**
- Prioritize: Magic Shoes → Mystical Armor → Cursed Helmet
- Add: Adaptive Glaive, Athena's Shield
- Boots: Magic Shoes or Tough Boots

**Against Healing-Heavy Teams:**
- Must Include: Sea Halberd, Cursed Helmet, or Glowing Wand
- Stops enemy sustain and healing effects
- Critical for assassins and fighters

**Against Tank-Heavy Teams:**
- Must Include: Demon Hunter Sword, Divine Glaive
- Armor pen and magic pen become priority
- Extends damage output duration

**Against Burst Damage Teams:**
- Prioritize Defense Items Early
- Consider: Immortality, Twilight Armor, Athena's Shield
- Focus on survival items before damage items
